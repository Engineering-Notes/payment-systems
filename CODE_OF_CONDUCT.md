# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a technical reference, and it works best when discussion stays precise, respectful and focused on the work. Everyone who participates in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space is expected to help keep it that way, regardless of background, experience level, nationality, gender, religion, age, disability or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique code and claims, never people.
- Back technical claims with evidence: a source, a specification or a reproducible run.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions before drawing conclusions.
- Accept correction gracefully, including corrections from less experienced contributors.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately off-topic content.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at mwangijoenjoroge@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
