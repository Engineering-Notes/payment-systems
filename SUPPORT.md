# Support

- **Questions about an article:** comment on the article at [codemwangi.substack.com](https://codemwangi.substack.com).
- **Code that fails or gives different results:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/JosephNjorog/engineering-notes/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Errors in an article:** open an [article correction](https://github.com/JosephNjorog/engineering-notes/issues/new?template=article_correction.yml).
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This is a personal project maintained on a best-effort basis.
